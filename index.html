Case Studies

How I work, not just what I ship.

Two deep dives - one from production analytics work, one from the Tableau Public community.

Analytics Engineering · LexHive

Rebuilding a reporting stack that couldn't keep up

Problem
10 production dashboards fed by per-team extracts from 10 different source systems. Teams saw conflicting versions of the same metrics, transformation logic lived inside Tableau workbooks, and the heaviest views took over 2 minutes to load.
Approach
Moved transformation logic out of workbooks and into Git-versioned dbt staging and mart models in Amazon Redshift. Standardised statuses and timestamps across campaign, lead-source, and pipeline data, and wrote scoped metric specifications with each consuming team before rebuilding.
Result: load times dropped from 2+ minutes to 15 seconds (8×), and 6 teams now share one governed version of every metric.
Data Visualisation · Makeover Monday

UFO sightings, redesigned as flight paths

Problem
60,000+ NUFORC sighting records across the contiguous US - a dataset usually shown as a plain dot map that hides shape categories and patterns in the noise.
Approach
Engineered custom sigmoid-interpolated flight paths in Tableau, densifying the data so each sighting shape category traces its own curved trajectory across the map - turning a static scatter into a narrative graphic, built entirely with native Tableau calculations.
Result: an advanced-technique viz demonstrating data densification, table calculations, and custom map design.
